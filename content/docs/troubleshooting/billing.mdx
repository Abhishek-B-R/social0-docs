## Overview

Cloud billing runs through our payment provider (Stripe and/or Dodo, depending on region and checkout). Most billing UI lives at **Dashboard → Billing**. Self-host deployments do not use Social0 cloud billing.

Start here: [Billing FAQ](/docs/billing/faq) · [Plans](/docs/billing/plans) · [Fair usage](/docs/billing/fair-usage).

## Payment blocked or checkout fails

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| Checkout never loads / spinner forever | Ad or tracker blocker | Disable adblock for social0.app and the payment provider domain; try another browser |
| Card declined | Bank or card rules | Try another card; contact your bank; ensure billing country matches card |
| 3-D Secure / authentication loop | Popup blocked | Allow popups; complete bank verification |
| Discount or coupon fails | Code expired or wrong plan | Re-enter code on checkout; confirm the code applies to the plan you chose |

## Blank or broken billing page

1. Hard refresh **Dashboard → Billing**
2. Disable adblock and privacy extensions
3. Sign out and sign in again
4. Try a different browser or network

If the page loads but plan is wrong, wait a few seconds after payment and refresh again (webhook delay).

## Plan not updating after payment

Normal flow:

1. Choose plan and complete checkout with payment provider
2. Return to Social0 — plan should show active

If it still shows Free or the old plan after ~1 minute:

- Refresh Billing
- Confirm the charge email matches your Social0 account email
- Email [support@social0.app](mailto:support@social0.app) with charge date and receipt (no full card number)

See [Billing FAQ — charged but plan didn’t update](/docs/billing/faq).

## Trial / subscription ended — posts fail with "Payment required"

When a paid subscription ends (or free lifetime posts are exhausted), publish/schedule can fail with a payment-required style message.

1. **Dashboard → Billing** — upgrade or add payment method
2. Confirm plan shows active (or free posts remaining on Free)
3. Retry the failed post from **Posts**
2. Retry failed posts from **Posts** after the plan is active

Details: [Billing FAQ — trial ended](/docs/billing/faq).

## Cancel, change plan, refunds

- **Change plan:** Billing page → select another plan
- **Cancel:** Manage subscription on Billing (may redirect to payment provider)
- **Refunds:** Policy in [Terms](/docs/terms) and [social0.app/terms](https://social0.app/terms). Contact support with account email and reason.

After cancel, you keep access until the period ends, then drop to **Free** (up to **3** accounts, **10** lifetime posts).

## Tips

- Keep your payment method current so your plan does not lapse unexpectedly
- Teams collaboration requires **Pro** on the owner’s workspace — [Teams](/docs/dashboard/teams)
- Usage limits (accounts, tweets/month) are plan-based — [Plans](/docs/billing/plans)
