## Overview

How many social accounts you can connect depends on your **plan**. Free includes a small connection cap and a lifetime free-post quota. Paid plans raise the account cap and remove the free-post limit.

Full comparison: [Plans](/docs/billing/plans) · [Connecting accounts](/docs/getting-started/connecting-accounts).

## Free vs paid

| Tier | Connected accounts | Publish / schedule |
|------|--------------------|--------------------|
| **Free** | Up to **3** | Up to **10** lifetime posts (drafts do not count against the quota) |
| **Starter** | Up to **5** | Unlimited posts (plan features apply) |
| **Growth** | Up to **15** | Unlimited posts + growth tools |
| **Pro** | Up to **50** | Unlimited posts + teams |

When the free-post quota is used up, Social0 blocks **Post now** and **Schedule** until you upgrade. Saving drafts still works.

## "Max accounts reached"

You hit your plan’s connection limit. Social0 blocks new connects until you free a slot or upgrade.

**Free a slot:**

1. **Dashboard → Connections**
2. **Disconnect** an account you no longer need
3. Connect the new account

Disconnecting removes Social0’s access to post on that account; it does not delete the account on the platform.

**Upgrade for more slots:**

- Free → Starter (3 → 5)
- Starter → Growth (5 → 15)
- Growth → Pro (15 → 50)

Open **Billing** to change plan.

## Multiple accounts on one platform

You can connect more than one account per network (e.g. two Twitter accounts). Each connection counts toward your limit. When using the API, CLI, or MCP, pass the account **UUID** if multiple accounts share a platform — names alone can be ambiguous. See [API errors](/docs/api/errors).

## Plan lapse

If a paid plan ends, you return to Free limits (3 accounts active, free-post quota). Extra connections may be marked inactive until you upgrade again. See [Billing troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/billing).

## Tips

- Disconnect unused accounts before connecting new ones on smaller plans
- Workspace/team limits are separate from connection count — [Teams](/docs/dashboard/teams)
- Twitter/X monthly post quotas on paid plans are separate from the free lifetime post quota — [Plans](/docs/billing/plans)
