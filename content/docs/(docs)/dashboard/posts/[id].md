## Overview

When you click a post from the Posts list, you open the **post detail** page. You see the full content, media, status per platform, and actions to edit, retry, delete, or post again.

## How the page is laid out

Two columns.

**Left**

1. **Post content**
2. **Media** (if the post has images or video)
3. Under Media:
   - If **X is not** on the post: a full-width **Post analytics** card (published or partial posts).
   - If **X is** on the post: that row splits — **Post analytics** | **Auto-Plug & Auto-Repost** (Growth and Pro; X-only). A scheduled X post can show Auto-Plug / Auto-Repost without the analytics card.

**Post analytics** starts collapsed. Click **Show analytics** to load numbers. They do not load when the page opens. After that you can **Hide analytics** or **Refresh**.

**Right**

1. **Status card** — type badge (Text / Image / Video / Thread / Collection), Posted / Scheduled / Queued / Publishing / Partial / Failed / Draft, then **Post again** / **Edit and post** (and publish, retry, or delete when relevant), then Created / Scheduled or Queued / Posted times
2. **Publish status** — the per-platform publish log
3. **Platforms** — account rows, plus **View** on the network when published, or **Retry** if that account failed

## How to open a post

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Posts**.
2. Click the post you want.

You’ll see the full caption, media, and a status for each account you selected (e.g. “Posted,” “Failed,” “Scheduled”).

## What you can do

- **Edit** - Change the text, media, or schedule. For drafts, you’re taken to the create form. For scheduled posts, you can change the time or the content.
- **Publish now** - If the post is a draft or scheduled, you can publish it immediately.
- **Retry** - If one or more platforms failed, use **Retry** to send again to those platforms.
- **Post again** - For a post that’s already published (or partially published), use “Post again” or “Edit and post” to reuse the same content and send it again (e.g. with a new schedule).
- **Delete** - Remove the post. Use this for drafts or scheduled posts you no longer want.
- **View on platform** - In the Platforms list, published accounts get a **View** link on the network.
- **Show analytics** - Loads live views, likes, comments, and engagement for this post from the platforms (not stored). Same live platforms as [Analytics](/docs/dashboard/analytics) (X, Bluesky, YouTube, TikTok, and Pinterest today). Other networks on the post are skipped until they are live.

## Status per platform

Each connected account you chose for the post will show a status: Posted, Failed, Scheduled, Publishing, or Pending. If it failed, we’ll show a short reason when we can (e.g. “Payment required,” “Connection expired”). Fix the issue (e.g. upgrade, reconnect) and click **Retry**.

## Tips

- If you see “Payment required,” upgrade your plan in **Billing** and then retry.
- For TikTok, the post may take a few minutes to show on your profile; that’s normal.
- Analytics on this page wait for **Show analytics**. The dashboard [Analytics](/docs/dashboard/analytics) page is the overview across posts.
- Auto-Plug and Auto-Repost stay **X-only** and **Growth / Pro**. See [Auto-repost](/docs/features/auto-repost).

## Common questions

**Q: Can I edit a post after it’s published?**  
A: You can’t change what’s already on the platforms. You can use “Post again” or “Edit and post” to create a new post with the same (or edited) content.

**Q: Why does it say “Queued”?**  
A: The post is scheduled and assigned to a queue slot. It will go out in that time window. You can still edit or delete it before that time.

**Q: Why don’t analytics numbers appear right away?**  
A: The card is collapsed on purpose. Click **Show analytics** to fetch. If X isn’t on the post, that card is full width; if X is on the post, it sits beside Auto-Plug & Auto-Repost.
