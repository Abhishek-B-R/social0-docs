## Overview

Social0 keeps the service reliable with **plan limits**, **API hourly rate limits**, a short **anti-automation guard** when posting to Twitter/X very quickly, and each network’s own rules.

## What Social0 limits

| Limit | Where it applies |
|-------|------------------|
| **Connected accounts** | Free 3 · Starter 5 · Growth 15 · Pro 50 — see [Plans](/docs/billing/plans) |
| **Lifetime posts (Free)** | Up to **10** publish/schedule actions; drafts do not count |
| **API requests / hour** | Free 60 · Starter 300 · Growth 1,000 · Pro 5,000 — see [Rate limits](/docs/api/rate-limits) |
| **Fast X posting** | If you publish to Twitter/X too quickly, Social0 may ask you to wait a few minutes |

## Platform limits (outside Social0)

LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, X, and others enforce their own rate limits and policies. If a publish fails with a platform rate-limit or policy error, wait and retry, or check that network’s help center.

## Tips

- Hitting Free’s **10** lifetime posts? Upgrade from **Dashboard → Billing**.
- Hitting API **429**? Slow down or upgrade for a higher hourly quota.
- Publishing many posts to X in a short burst? Space them out if Social0 asks you to wait.
- For plan feature gates (bulk tools, resurface, teams), see [Plans](/docs/billing/plans).

## Common questions

**Q: What does Billing show for usage?**  
A: Your current plan and connected accounts versus your plan limit. Free also tracks lifetime posts used.

**Q: Do LinkedIn or Instagram posts count toward Free’s post limit?**  
A: Yes. Free’s **10** lifetime posts count every publish or schedule on any platform. Drafts do not count. Paid plans remove that lifetime cap.

**Q: What if X blocks or rate-limits my account?**  
A: That comes from X. Wait, reduce frequency, or check X’s own limits for your account type.
