## Overview

Besides creating and publishing posts, Social0 lets you schedule for later, use a queue for consistent posting, upload multiple images or videos at once (bulk tools), and-on Growth and Pro plans-resurface or auto-plug posts. **Analytics** and **Inbox** are early access on every plan (flask in the sidebar) for platforms that are live today. This section explains each feature in plain terms.

## Features at a glance

- **Scheduling** - Pick a date and time; Social0 publishes the post for you then. Available on all paid plans.
- **Queue** - Set up time slots (e.g. “every day at 9 AM”) and assign scheduled posts to them. Your posts go out in those windows.
- **Bulk tools** - Upload and schedule many images or videos in one go. Available on Growth and Pro.
- **Auto-repost** - Resurface (re-post or boost) and auto-plug (add a plug to high-performing posts). Available on Growth and Pro. X-only.
- **Analytics** - Live views, likes, comments, and engagement for posts you published through Social0. **X, Bluesky, YouTube, TikTok, and Pinterest** today; Meta and LinkedIn coming. See [Analytics](/docs/dashboard/analytics).
- **Inbox** - Comments on those posts (X, Bluesky, YouTube today), plus DMs on X and Bluesky. See [Inbox](/docs/dashboard/inbox).
- **Teams & Workspaces** - Organize connections across workspaces; on **Pro**, invite admins and members to collaborate. See [Teams & Workspaces](/docs/dashboard/teams).

## How to use Analytics and Inbox

1. Connect a live account from **Connections** (Analytics: X, Bluesky, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest. Inbox comments: X, Bluesky, YouTube. Inbox DMs: X, Bluesky).
2. Publish through Social0 as usual.
3. Open **Dashboard** → **Analytics** for totals and top posts, or **Inbox** to reply to comments and DMs.
4. Use the same date chips on both pages: **7D**, **2W**, **4W**, **3M**, **1Y**, or a custom range.

A flask next to those items in the sidebar means early access, not a different product.

## How to use scheduling

1. Create your post (Composer or Create).
2. When you choose when to publish, select **Schedule** instead of **Publish now**.
3. Pick the date and time (we use the timezone in your Settings).
4. Confirm. The post appears in your Posts list as **Scheduled** and will go out at that time.

## How to use the queue

1. Go to **Settings** → **Queue** and set up your slots (e.g. which days and times).
2. When you schedule a post, you can assign it to one of those slots instead of a fixed time.
3. The post will go out in that time window. Scheduled posts that are in a queue show as **Queued** in your Posts list.

## Tips

- Set your timezone in **Settings** → **Preferences** so scheduled times are correct.
- Bulk tools save time when you have many images or videos to post over several days.
- Analytics and Inbox are early access (flask in the sidebar), not extra plans. Analytics is live on five networks; Inbox comments and DMs each have a smaller live set. We're waiting on access from Meta and LinkedIn.
