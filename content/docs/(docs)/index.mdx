## Overview

Social0 is a **multi-platform social scheduler for creators and AI agents**, hosted at [social0.app](https://social0.app). Write once and publish everywhere — create a post in one place, pick which accounts get it, then publish now or schedule for later.

You can connect Twitter/X, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Bluesky, Threads, Facebook, and Pinterest. One dashboard. One composer. Your content, your schedule.

## How to get started

1. Go to [social0.app](https://social0.app) and sign up (Google or email).
2. Verify your email if you used email sign-up.
3. Choose a plan or skip for now — on **Free** you can connect up to **3** accounts and publish or schedule up to **10** lifetime posts (drafts do not count).
4. Open **Dashboard** → **Connections** and connect your first account.
5. Open the **Composer**, write your post, add media if you like, then publish or schedule.

See [Getting started](/docs/getting-started) for a fuller walkthrough, and [How it works](/docs/how-it-works) for the big picture.

## What you can do

- **Create posts** — Text, images, videos, threads, and collection posts.
- **Schedule** — Set a date and time; Social0 publishes for you.
- **Analytics** — See how posts you published through Social0 are doing (**X and Bluesky only today**; other networks are waiting on access we've requested). Early access — flask in the sidebar.
- **Inbox** — Reply to comments and DMs on those live accounts. Same flask; unread count in the nav.
- **Manage connections** — Add or remove accounts from Connections (per workspace).
- **Teams & Workspaces** — Organize accounts; on Pro, invite teammates. See [Teams & Workspaces](/docs/dashboard/teams).
- **Build with the API** — [REST API](/docs/api/quickstart) and [per-platform settings](/docs/api/providers).
- **Use the CLI** — Publish from the terminal with [`social0`](/docs/cli).
- **Manage with AI** — Claude, Cursor, or ChatGPT via the [MCP Server](/docs/mcp).
- **Get unstuck** — [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting) and [Support](/docs/support).

## Tips

- Start with one or two connected accounts so you can see how posting and scheduling work.
- Use the Composer for a quick start — it suggests the right post type based on what you add.
- Your timezone in Settings is used for all scheduled times.

## Common questions

**Q: Do I need to pay to try Social0?**  
A: No. **Free** lets you connect up to **3** accounts and publish or schedule up to **10** lifetime posts (drafts do not count). Upgrade from **Dashboard** → **Billing** when you need more.

**Q: Can I change or cancel my plan later?**  
A: Yes. Go to **Dashboard** → **Billing**. See [Billing](/docs/billing/plans) for details.

**Q: Where do I connect my accounts?**  
A: **Dashboard** → **Connections**. Click the platform you want and follow the sign-in steps.
