## Overview

Your subscription sets how many accounts you can connect, whether you have bulk/growth tools, and — on **Pro** — whether you can run **collaborative Teams**. **Free** includes up to **3** connected accounts and **10** lifetime posts (drafts do not count). Billing is always **personal**, even when you’re working in a team. Team collaboration depends on the **owner’s** Pro plan. See [Plans](/docs/billing/plans) and [Teams & Workspaces](/docs/dashboard/teams).

Manage everything from **Dashboard → Billing**.

## How to open Billing

1. In the Dashboard, open the sidebar or menu.
2. Click **Billing** (or **Subscription**).
3. You’ll see your current plan, how many accounts you’ve connected, and (for some plans) how many tweets you’ve sent this month.

## What you can do on the Billing page

- **See your plan** - Free, Starter, Growth, or Pro, and what’s included.
- **Upgrade** - Choose a paid plan and complete payment. You’re sent to our payment provider; when you’re done, you come back to Social0 and your new plan is active.
- **Change plan** - Switch to a different paid plan (e.g. Starter to Growth).
- **Cancel** - Cancel your subscription. What happens to your data and access is described in our Terms and Privacy Policy.

## Tips

- If you just upgraded and the page still shows the old plan, wait a few seconds and refresh. Sometimes it takes a moment to update.
- Keep your payment method up to date so you don’t lose access by accident.

## Common questions

**Q: Where do I cancel?**  
A: On the Billing page, use the option to cancel or manage subscription. You may be taken to our payment provider to confirm.

**Q: What happens when my paid plan ends?**  
A: You drop to **Free**: up to **3** connected accounts and **10** lifetime posts (drafts do not count). Existing connections stay, but publish/schedule may fail if you’ve used all free posts or exceed the account cap. Sign in and upgrade from **Billing** to restore paid limits. See our [Terms](https://social0.app/terms) and [Privacy Policy](https://social0.app/privacy) for details.
